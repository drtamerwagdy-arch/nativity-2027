Team guide · how to use your team page

Your link

  • Your personal link is your key: keep it to yourself. Open it once on your phone and the page remembers you.
  • Save it to your home screen: iPhone Share → Add to Home Screen; Android ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  • Lost or changed your phone? Tell Tamer. He makes you a new link and the old one stops working.

1Selling tickets on the spot

  1. Under New sale, type the buyer's first and last name and email (mobile is optional).
  2. Printed tickets handed over: type their numbers (e.g. 12-14). Hand them out from No. 1 upward; the page shows the highest number you can use.
    Email e-tickets: choose how many; they arrive by email.
  3. Enter the amount received, choose Cash or Cheque, and submit.
  4. Buyer doesn't need a tax receipt? Tick Buyer declines a tax receipt.

Made a mistake? Undo it under My sales within 15 minutes.

2Collecting a cash booking

  1. The buyer shows the QR code from their booking email, or their code (like WW27-0007).
  2. Tap Scan code and point at the QR, or type the code and tap Find.
  3. Check the name and the amount, collect the cash, tap Paid. Their tickets are emailed straight away.

Bookings hold seats for 10 days. After that the seats go back on sale.

3At the door

  1. Tap Scan code and point at the ticket's door code (in the ticket email) or the QR on a printed ticket. Typing T377 works too.
  2. ✓ registered: welcome them in. Not registered: ask Tamer.

4Your cash

  • Cash you hold (top right) adds up what you collected.
  • Give the cash to Hany (treasurer). Tamer records the hand-over and your total goes back down.
