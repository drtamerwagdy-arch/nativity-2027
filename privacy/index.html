Privacy policy

ST. MINA COPTIC ORTHODOX CHURCH, CALGARY, AB · WONDER WORKER ART TEAM

This policy covers the St. Mina Calgary app (iPhone and Android) and the thewonderworker.ca pages (tickets, donations, sponsorships and The Wonder Worker Book Store) run by the Wonder Worker Art Team of St. Mina Coptic Orthodox Church, Calgary.

The app

The app has no advertising and no tracking, and we run no analytics. It reads public church information (announcements, service times, readings, event details, seats left). To save you typing, the book store keeps your basket, and the name, e-mail and phone you entered, on your own phone for 7 days. When you order, give or get tickets in the app, your details (below) go to the church's server so your order can be prepared. Card details are typed on Square's secure page and never pass through the app or the church's server.

Tickets, donations and sponsorships

When you buy tickets or give, we ask for your name, e-mail address, and optionally your phone number and postal address (for a tax receipt). We use these only to send your tickets, to issue your tax receipt, and to contact you about your order. If you pay by e-Transfer, the screenshot you upload is used only to confirm your payment.

The Wonder Worker Book Store

When you order from the book store, on the website or in the app, we ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number and preferred pick-up time, and you may add a note. We use these only to prepare your order, to e-mail your receipt, to tell you when it is ready to pick up, and to handle an exchange or refund. Card details are typed on Square's secure page; the church never sees your card number. Your name, e-mail and phone are removed 30 days after the order is completed or refunded.

Children and photos

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. To have a photo or video of your child removed, e-mail us and we will take it down.

Who sees it

Only authorized church personnel handling tickets, tax receipts and book store orders. We never sell or share your details, and we never send marketing you did not ask for.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Records are kept in the church's Google account, which may be outside Canada. Ticket, donation and sponsorship details are kept until tax receipts for the year are issued and the church's record-keeping duties are met, then deleted. Book store orders are kept for the church's sales records, without your name, e-mail or phone once the 30 days have passed.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Square (see Square's privacy policy); Interac e-Transfers by your bank. We receive only the confirmation that a payment was made.

Other services

Daily readings are loaded from katameros.app, and videos open on YouTube. Forms from the church website (stminacalgary.ca) shown in the app, such as Prayer Request, are handled by that website.

Your choices and questions

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, e-mail artteam@stminacalgary.ca.
