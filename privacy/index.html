Privacy policy

ST. MINA COPTIC ORTHODOX CHURCH, CALGARY, AB · WONDER WORKER ART TEAM

This policy covers the St. Mina Calgary iPhone app and the thewonderworker.ca pages (tickets, donations, sponsorships) run by the Wonder Worker Art Team of St. Mina Coptic Orthodox Church, Calgary.

The app

The app stores nothing about you on your phone and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. It reads public information from the church's server (announcements, event details, seats left). Tickets, donations and sponsorships open the church's own web pages inside the app; card details are typed on Square's secure page and never pass through the app or the church's server.

Tickets, donations and sponsorships

When you buy tickets or give, we ask for your name, e-mail address, and optionally your phone number and postal address (for a tax receipt). We use these only to send your tickets, to issue your tax receipt, and to contact you about your order. If you pay by e-Transfer and upload a screenshot, it is checked and then deleted; anything personal that is not needed (account or card numbers, balances) is never kept.

Who sees it

Only authorized church personnel handling tickets and tax receipts. We never sell or share your details, and we never send marketing you did not ask for.

How long we keep it

Order details are kept until tax receipts for the year are issued and the church's record-keeping duties are met, then deleted.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Square (see Square's privacy policy); Interac e-Transfers by your bank. We receive only the confirmation that a payment was made.

Your choices and questions

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, e-mail artteam@stminacalgary.ca.
